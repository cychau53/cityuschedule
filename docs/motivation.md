\# Motivation



CityU students often need to compare several lectures, tutorials, and

laboratory sections before finalizing their timetable.



A proposed timetable may look acceptable at first, but it can contain:



\- Overlapping classes

\- Inconvenient gaps between classes

\- Back-to-back sessions in different buildings

\- A schedule that is difficult to import into a personal calendar



`cityuschedule` reduces this planning friction. Students provide a simple CSV

file containing the classes they are considering, and the package checks the

schedule before they finalize their course selection.



The goal is to turn a list of class sessions into a clear, actionable weekly

timetable.
